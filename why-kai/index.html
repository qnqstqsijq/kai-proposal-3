The workflow is the point.

The point is not a longer list of tools. It is one flow: you set the goal, an agent prepares the move, you see the rate and fees, and you approve.

  1. You set the goal

    Pay an approved supplier within budget.

  2. The agent prepares

    It checks balances and proposes how to fund the payment.

  3. You confirm

    You review the amount, route and fees, and approve in Kai platform.

  4. It goes out

    The payment is sent through Kai platform's payment providers.

A budget or permission never replaces your confirmation of a transfer.
